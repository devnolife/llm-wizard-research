        Full Report Body
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        Introduction
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Research-gap detection across a corpus is shifting away from abstract-only mapping toward 
      
      
        
          
        
        full-text, provenance-linked, hybrid pipelines
      
      
        
         that combine LLM extraction, retrieval, and knowledge graphs [34] [56] [57]. In the supplied evidence, the strongest recent pattern is not a single dominant algorithm but a family of systems that operationalize a “gap” as one of the following: a 
      
      
        
          
        
        missing relation
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        unretrievable support
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        ontology-level coverage failure
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        niche topic discarded as an outlier
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        semantically distant novelty
      
      
        
        , or 
      
      
        
          
        
        under-described workflow step
      
      
        
         [22] [25] [26] [28] [31] [49].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Taken together, these findings suggest that the newest practical methods are best understood as 
      
      
        
          
        
        candidate-gap generation plus validation layers
      
      
        
        , rather than as closed-form end-to-end gap detectors [7] [38] [58].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Confidence level: High
      
      
        
         - this synthesis is directly supported across the meta-analysis and the method-specific findings [7] [37] [43].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        Foundational Context
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        A corpus-scale gap detector needs at least four layers:
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Evidence extraction from papers
      
      
        
        : parse PDF text, segment it, preserve provenance [34] [59].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Normalization into a structured representation
      
      
        
        : entities, relations, sections, workflows, or ontology-aligned concepts [2] [60].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Gap operationalization
      
      
        
        : decide whether the gap is a missing edge, missing support, missing coverage, or missing method step [25] [31] [49].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Validation/ranking
      
      
        
        : use link prediction, constraint checking, retrieval support, entropy gating, or novelty scores to prioritize candidates [21] [22] [26] [28].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        This matters because the excluded baselines in the query mostly work on 
      
      
        
          
        
        topic geometry, citation structure, contradiction pairs, or aspect checklists
      
      
        
        , whereas the newer methods rely on 
      
      
        
          
        
        structured evidence and cross-paper reasoning
      
      
        
         [37] [61] [62].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        1. Full-Text PDF Parsing and Claim Extraction Pipelines
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Recent full-text pipelines generally follow a common sequence: 
      
      
        
          
        
        PDF conversion/parsing, segmentation, extraction/filtering, normalization, and provenance-linked storage
      
      
        
         [34] [59]. One LLM-assisted KG pipeline stores sentence-level evidence tied to normalized entities, explicitly preserving provenance for later reasoning [34]. Another family uses direct PDF parsing to preserve context instead of manual page-by-page segmentation [35].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        1.1 MAKES-QA
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Preprocess each document, extract first-page research topics, split full text into overlapping page-level chunks, summarize chunk relevance, run entity extraction and relation extraction, then assemble normalized triples [4].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Full-text PDFs [4].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / rules.
      
      
        
         At most 
      
      
        
          
        
        10 entities
      
      
        
         and 
      
      
        
          
        
        5 high-confidence relations per chunk
      
      
        
         [4].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         No precision/recall/F1 is reported in the supplied excerpt [4].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         It ignores figures, tables, and mathematical content; chunk-level truncation can lose evidence distributed across modalities or long contexts [4].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        1.2 SCS-KG
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Three-step pipeline: entity/relation extraction, entity/relation normalization, and triple verification before graph insertion [2].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Full text plus normalization resources or ontology support [2].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / rules.
      
      
        
         A verification gate is present, but no numeric cutoff is exposed [2].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Not reported in the excerpt [2].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Incomplete graphs and noisy extraction remain possible despite verification [7].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        1.3 PKG-LLM
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Extract entities and relations from full text, build a domain KG, then run link prediction to surface absent or weakly supported links [3].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Full documents plus domain ontology or identifiers [3].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / rules.
      
      
        
         No explicit decision threshold is given [3].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Entity extraction F1 
      
      
        
          
        
        0.93
      
      
        
        , relation extraction F1 
      
      
        
          
        
        0.90
      
      
        
        , link-prediction AUC 
      
      
        
          
        
        0.92
      
      
        
         on 
      
      
        
          
        
        430 documents
      
      
        
         [3].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Literature-derived predictions may be plausible yet unsafe without domain constraints [21].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        1.4 MCRAH-GIE
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Multi-tier gap validation with weighted impact scoring plus section-credibility and coherence checks, using chunk-, paragraph-, and document-level hierarchical embeddings; it also validates methodological relations, gap associations, authorship, domains, and concepts as graph edges, with temporal gap forecasting from KG link prediction and embedding evolution [1].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Full-text PDFs, extracted entities/relations, section metadata, hierarchical embeddings, KG [1].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / rules.
      
      
        
         The exact weighted gap formula is not exposed; a related hierarchical clustering stage uses methodology score 
      
      
        
          
        
        >0.3
      
      
        
         [1].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         No end-to-end metric is shown in the supplied excerpt [1].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Shallow graph semantics and missed cross-document links still require expert review [6] [7].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: Medium
      
      
        
         - the extraction pipelines are well supported, but many scoring details are unpublished in the excerpts [1] [2] [4].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        2. Knowledge Graph Construction and Missing-Edge Detection
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        The KG-based literature treats research gaps primarily as 
      
      
        
          
        
        missing entities or missing relations
      
      
        
         rather than as missing mechanisms or properties [49] [50]. Scholarly KGs are typically built from entities, relations, and coreference links extracted from chunks or documents [60] [63] [64].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        2.1 Open-world and inductive KGC
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm family.
      
      
        
         Methods such as 
      
      
        
          
        
        EmReCo, MANNER, BERT-FKGC, MuCo-KGC, BiRMet, and PReSA
      
      
        
         map text descriptions, schema, or context into graph space and score unseen entity-relation candidates; some add contrastive relation-context learning or schema augmentation for unseen relations [8] [9] [10] [11] [12] [13].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Literature-derived KG, textual descriptions of entities/relations, schema/context, sometimes multimodal descriptions [8] [9] [11].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / scoring.
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        ASLEEP and ShallowBKGC use 
      
      
        
          
        
        per-relation probabilities
      
      
        
         via multi-label modeling [14].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        ClueKGC narrows the 
      
      
        
          
        
        score gap
      
      
        
         between positives and negatives using negative clues [15].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        KG-MPP boosts entity scores for candidates judged more likely correct [16].
      
      
        
        
      
      
        
        No retrieved paper supplies a universal threshold for declaring a relation a “gap” [17] [42].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Standard KGC metrics dominate, especially 
      
      
        
          
        
        MRR
      
      
        
         and 
      
      
        
          
        
        Hits@K
      
      
        
        ; MuCo-KGC reports gains on FB15k-237, WN18RR, and CoDEx benchmarks [10].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Dependence on low-quality descriptions, hallucinated external knowledge, weak relation modeling, and poor generalization when both unseen entities and unseen relations appear together [10] [12] [18] [19] [20].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        2.2 Constraint-aware neuro-symbolic KGC
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Predict links, then validate them against ontology constraints using SHACL to separate 
      
      
        
          
        
        PCA_valid
      
      
        
         from 
      
      
        
          
        
        PCA_invalid
      
      
        
         candidates [21].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         KG plus ontology/constraints [21].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / scoring.
      
      
        
         Binary validity by constraint satisfaction, not probability cutoff [21].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Improved MRR and Hits@K on 
      
      
        
          
        
        315 testbeds
      
      
        
         [21].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         The method exists because unconstrained link prediction can elevate medically or scientifically unsafe relations that merely look plausible [21].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        2.3 From missing link to gap hypothesis
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Taken together, these findings imply a practical rule: 
      
      
        
          
        
        do not treat a high-scoring missing edge as a research gap unless it is also constraint-valid and supported by retrievable text evidence
      
      
        
         [21] [22]. This is an inference from multiple papers rather than a published single formula.
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: High
      
      
        
         - the method family and its limitations are consistent across the KG findings [9] [21] [49].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        3. LLM-Guided Gap Scoring and Novelty Ranking
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        The supplied evidence shows that LLM-based ranking is strongest for 
      
      
        
          
        
        ordering candidates
      
      
        
        , not for making calibrated final decisions [38] [65].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        3.1 Semantic novelty scoring
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        SemNovel.
      
      
        
         Scores each article by its 
      
      
        
          
        
        distance from prior publications in LLM-embedding space
      
      
        
        ; the score correlates with future citation impact [28]. The excerpt does not expose the full formula [28].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Recombinant novelty.
      
      
        
         Compute document vectors for all references, then take the 
      
      
        
          
        
        maximum pairwise cosine distance
      
      
        
         among references as the novelty score [29].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Paper/reference embeddings, sometimes citation metadata [28] [29].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         These methods appear better at adjacent novelty than radical paradigm shifts [30].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        3.2 Iterative LLM ranking and stopping rules
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        One literature review/classification loop stops when 
      
      
        
          
        
        LLM confidence > 0.9
      
      
        
        , with a hard cap of 
      
      
        
          
        
        five iterations
      
      
        
         [66]. Another retrieval ranking setup uses the probability of 
      
      
        
          
        
        YES/NO
      
      
        
         for binary relevance generation, while query-generation scoring uses the conditional probability of the query token sequence given a retrieved document [67]. These are ranking controls, but they are not published as research-gap formulas [66] [67].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        3.3 Why raw confidence is a weak gap signal
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        LLM extraction pipelines often emit self-reported confidence in JSON outputs [68] [69] [70]. But one biomedical system found confidence was “high” in over 
      
      
        
          
        
        98%
      
      
        
         of cases and did not separate correct from incorrect outputs [38]. Another benchmark found overconfidence was common, though confidence still helped route cases for human review [39].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Therefore, a practical implication is that 
      
      
        
          
        
        self-rated LLM confidence should be used for triage, not as the primary gap score
      
      
        
         [38] [39].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: High
      
      
        
         - the evidence is clear that ranking exists, but calibration is weak [38] [39].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        4. Retrieval-Augmented Synthesis for Underexplored Concepts
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        RAG-style systems contribute to gap detection by identifying 
      
      
        
          
        
        missing support
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        underexplored mechanism combinations
      
      
        
        , or 
      
      
        
          
        
        queries that fail to retrieve evidence
      
      
        
         [22] [71].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        4.1 Large-scale retrieval systems
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        OpenScholar retrieves from 
      
      
        
          
        
        45 million open-access papers
      
      
        
         and generates citation-backed answers; OpenScholar-8B outperformed GPT-4o and PaperQA2 on correctness, while GPT-4o hallucinated citations 
      
      
        
          
        
        78-90%
      
      
        
         of the time in that evaluation [46]. ColBERT and RETRO exemplify large-scale retrieval architectures built for millions of documents or chunk databases [72] [73].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        4.2 Retrieval-failure as a gap signal
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        The Graph-RAG evaluation framework scores support based on 
      
      
        
          
        
        retrieval-hit availability
      
      
        
         and whether the model behaves appropriately when evidence is missing [22]. Under graph degradation, performance drops from 
      
      
        
          
        
        99.20
      
      
        
         to 
      
      
        
          
        
        46.50
      
      
        
        , showing that evidence accessibility strongly shapes support detection [22].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        This is especially relevant for gap mining because an unsupported claim candidate may indicate either a 
      
      
        
          
        
        real coverage gap
      
      
        
         or a 
      
      
        
          
        
        retrieval/indexing failure
      
      
        
         [22] [71].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        4.3 Conflict: richer retrieval vs precision control
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Some systems argue that graph or multi-source retrieval improves completeness and cross-document linkage [74] [75] [76], while other evidence warns that multi-hop retrieval introduces too much irrelevant context and can hurt performance, motivating single-hop limits [77]. This is an explicit tradeoff between completeness and precision.
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: Medium
      
      
        
         - retrieval architectures are well supported, but explicit gap-ranking logic remains only partially specified [22] [46].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        5. Niche-Topic Preservation and Outlier Recovery
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        A notable recent shift is that gap detection increasingly treats 
      
      
        
          
        
        outlier recovery as essential
      
      
        
        , because low-density papers may represent precisely the emerging areas a gap detector should preserve [26].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        5.1 Centroid reassignment + entropy gating
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Reassign outlier documents geometrically back into topics, then compute ontology-derived entropy and route only ambiguous cases to an LLM [26].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Document embeddings, ontology/taxonomy, corpus text [26].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / scoring.
      
      
        
         Entropy gate is explicit, but the numeric cutoff is not provided [26].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Coverage rises from 
      
      
        
          
        
        75.5%±1.2%
      
      
        
         to 
      
      
        
          
        
        95.7%±0.4%
      
      
        
        ; centroid reassignment contributes 
      
      
        
          
        
        +22.9 percentage points
      
      
        
         and the full pipeline retains 
      
      
        
          
        
        69 statistically significant trends
      
      
        
         [26].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Dependence on ontology quality and weak cluster separation in exploratory corpora [27].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        5.2 Secondary analysis of low-frequency terms
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Another method keeps keywords with 
      
      
        
          
        
        fewer than five occurrences
      
      
        
         for secondary cluster analysis, explicitly to recover high-granularity directions obscured by main thresholds [78]. This is not a full PDF-to-KG pipeline, but it is a concrete anti-outlier safeguard [78].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: High
      
      
        
         - the niche-topic preservation trend is directly and repeatedly supported [26] [78].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        6. Workflow and Stage Mining as Gap Detection
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        A distinct family of methods looks for gaps in 
      
      
        
          
        
        research process structure
      
      
        
         rather than in concepts [31].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Algorithm.
      
      
        
         Identify workflow-relevant paragraphs, distill step phrases, classify them into stages such as 
      
      
        
          
        
        data preparation
      
      
        
        , 
      
      
        
          
        
        data processing
      
      
        
        , and 
      
      
        
          
        
        data analysis
      
      
        
        , then reconstruct the workflow [31].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Inputs.
      
      
        
         Full-text PDFs, paragraph and sentence text [31].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Thresholds / scoring.
      
      
        
         No numeric acceptance threshold is reported [31].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Performance.
      
      
        
         Reported mainly qualitatively through workflow reconstruction; a related full-text gap coding study reports 
      
      
        
          
        
        Cohen’s kappa = 0.83
      
      
        
         for manual validation of identified gaps [32].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Failure modes.
      
      
        
         Text-only retrieval misses details scattered across tables, figures, and auxiliary experimental settings [33].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Taken together, these findings suggest that 
      
      
        
          
        
        workflow mining is one of the clearest routes beyond topic clustering
      
      
        
        , because it detects missing or under-described methodology rather than only underrepresented themes [31] [32].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: Medium
      
      
        
         - conceptually strong, but quantitative automation evidence is limited [31] [32].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        7. Graph Neural Networks and Link Prediction for Absent Scholarly Connections
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        GNN and KG link-prediction methods are not gap detectors by themselves, but they form an emerging substrate for finding 
      
      
        
          
        
        hidden scholarly connections
      
      
        
         [79] [80].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        7.1 Relevant architectures
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        GraphSAGE
      
      
        
        : inductive neighborhood sampling and aggregation, designed for unseen nodes and unseen graphs [81].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        ConvE
      
      
        
        : 2D convolution over entity/relation embeddings, parameter-efficient and strong on robust benchmarks [82].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        TransE
      
      
        
        : translation-based scoring for multi-relational prediction [83].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        node2vec / DeepWalk / LINE
      
      
        
        : large-scale graph embeddings; DeepWalk is especially strong when information is missing [84] [85] [86].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Unified contrastive / schema-augmented methods
      
      
        
         for unseen relations and sparse connectivity [87] [88].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        7.2 Scoring and thresholds
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        These methods typically produce 
      
      
        
          
        
        edge scores, rankings, or binary predictions
      
      
        
        , but the supplied evidence does not show a universal probability threshold for saying an absent edge is a research gap [89] [90] [91]. One paper does provide statistically rigorous 
      
      
        
          
        
        uncertainty estimates for edge probabilities
      
      
        
        , though not a simple fixed threshold rule [92].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        7.3 Validation against artifacts
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Important best practices include:
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        treat unobserved edges as 
      
      
        
          
        
        unlabeled rather than negative
      
      
        
         to reduce artifact bias [93];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        use 
      
      
        
          
        
        hard negatives
      
      
        
         rather than easy random negatives, as in HeaRT [94];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        verify missing links with retrieval evidence and human review, as in WikiRAG [95];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        audit for split leakage and benchmark design problems [54] [96].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: High
      
      
        
         - the adaptation path from link prediction to gap candidate generation is well supported, though not yet standardized for literature-gap benchmarks [89] [97].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        8. Cross-Method Synthesis: What Is Most Reproducible Now?
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        8.1 Best-supported reproducible pipeline
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        The most reproducible stack supported by the supplied evidence is:
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        PDF parser -> semantic chunking with overlap -> LLM/structured JSON extraction -> ontology/KG normalization -> triple verification -> inductive/open-world link prediction -> SHACL or ontology validation -> retrieval-support scoring -> uncertainty-gated human review
      
      
        
         [2] [3] [4] [9] [21] [22] [34] [38].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Why this stack stands out:
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        full-text provenance improves evidence coverage [34] [56];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        KGs reduce synonym ambiguity and improve traceability [7] [37];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        link prediction proposes non-obvious candidates [3] [9];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        constraint checks suppress unsafe false positives [21];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
        retrieval support distinguishes real gaps from unsupported hallucinations [22] [71].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        8.2 What still falls short
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        Across method families, the recurrent bottlenecks are:
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        missing explicit final scoring formulas
      
      
        
         [40] [42];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        weak calibration of LLM confidence
      
      
        
         [38];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        limited end-to-end gap metrics
      
      
        
         [42] [48];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        shallow or incomplete graphs
      
      
        
         [6] [98];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        difficulty preserving niche topics without special handling
      
      
        
         [26] [78];
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        multimodal omissions
      
      
        
         from tables, figures, and formulas [4] [33].
      
    
    

        
        
      
      
        
        8.3 Practical decision rule
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
        If your goal is immediate implementation on full-text PDFs, prioritize methods in this order:
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        PDF-to-KG extraction + verification + link prediction
      
      
        
         for broad reproducibility and strongest direct metrics [2] [3].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Constraint-aware KGC
      
      
        
         when false positives are costly [21].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Retrieval-failure scoring
      
      
        
         when you need evidence-grounded abstention [22].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Outlier recovery / entropy gating
      
      
        
         when preserving niche or emerging areas matters [26].
      
    
    

        
          
          
        
        
      
      
        
          
        
        Workflow-stage mining
      
      
        
         when the target gaps are methodological rather than conceptual [31].
      
    
    

        
      
      
        
          
        
        Section confidence: High
      
      
        
         - this ranking follows the reported reproducibility evidence and available metrics [1-3-1-10,2
